## Hello

M.Tech VLSI Design student. I got curious about how sand becomes a processor and never really stopped pulling that thread.

Most of my time goes into RTL work — writing Verilog that synthesis tools can actually handle cleanly, reading timing reports, understanding why a design closes or doesn't. I try to write RTL with physical constraints in mind from the start, not as something to sort out after simulation passes. Right now I'm deep into RISC-V microarchitecture, DDR4 memory controller design, and getting better at SDC constraint authoring.

I have 79 repos and counting — most of it is implementations built multiple ways on purpose, to see what the tools do differently.
